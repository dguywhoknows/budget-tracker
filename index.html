Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free. Each account gets a daily allowance of AI requests.

Where is my data stored?

What you create stays in this browser on this device. Your account is only used to sign you in and to use the built-in AI. You can export a backup or delete everything from Settings.

Do I need an API key?

No. Signed-in users get the built-in AI with nothing to set up. You can also connect your own Groq or OpenRouter key in Settings if you prefer.

What can I type into the AI box?

Anything you would ask a helpful colleague who knows the app, such as: "Set my dining budget to 300 and groceries to 450". It plans the steps, runs the app's own functions and tells you what it did. The Instructions tab saves preferences every AI feature follows.

Can I see the code?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license on GitHub, with a public test suite.
